# Product — Version 1

## Product Goal

A small business should be able to:

- set up its organization
- add users
- manage customers
- manage vendors
- manage products/services
- create quotations
- create sales orders
- generate invoices
- record customer payments
- create purchase orders
- receive goods
- record supplier bills
- record supplier payments
- track inventory
- record expenses
- view basic dashboards
- view essential reports
- import/export important data

## Version 1 Scope

Include:

- authentication
- organization setup
- fixed RBAC
- user management
- customers
- vendors
- products/services
- quotations
- sales orders
- invoices
- customer payments
- purchase orders
- goods receiving
- supplier bills
- supplier payments
- inventory
- stock adjustments
- expenses
- dashboard
- essential reports
- CSV import/export
- audit logs
- basic in-app indicators

## Roles

- owner
- manager
- employee

Roles are fixed in Version 1. There is no custom role builder.

## Explicit Non-Scope

The following are out of scope for Version 1:

- full accounting
- chart of accounts
- general ledger
- journal entries
- trial balance
- balance sheet
- payroll
- HR
- attendance
- advanced CRM
- manufacturing
- POS
- multi-currency
- advanced tax engine
- custom role builder
- native mobile apps
- AI features
- bank integrations
- bKash integrations
- WhatsApp integrations
- advanced workflow automation
- customer credit balance
- overpayment
- batch/serial/lot inventory tracking

## Core Product Principle

If a feature is not required for a small business to:

- sell
- purchase
- track inventory
- collect money
- pay vendors
- record expenses
- understand its basic business position

then it should normally be moved to Version 2.
